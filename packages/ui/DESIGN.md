# Design System: atm · Agent Tool Manager

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Quiet Control Room"**

The Quiet Control Room makes a dense local operations surface feel calm rather than compressed. Its voice is calm, precise, and restrained: solid neutral surfaces, hairline separators, compact controls, and state cues that can be scanned before they are read.

Signal Blue directs attention only where the operator must act, focus, or inspect a selected state. Graphite Mist supplies the page ground, six surface levels, four ink levels, and borders in both themes. Tool-type and status colors remain small, semantic signals rather than decoration.

Components feel refined and restrained. Depth comes from tonal layering at rest and from shadows only on transient overlays. Motion is brief and direct. Decorative gradients, content-layer glass, illustration-led empty states, and floating cards are rejected; the gradient in the ATM logo is binding brand artwork, not a content-style exception.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Dense information architecture with low visual noise.
- Solid Graphite Mist surfaces and precise 1px separators.
- One action accent, with semantic color reserved for compact status and tool-type cues.
- Compact system typography, monospace metadata, and tabular numerals.
- Flat static surfaces, floating transient overlays, and short functional motion.

## Colors

The palette pairs one clear action signal with a cool neutral ladder and tightly scoped semantic colors. Light values use the semantic keys; their `dark-` counterparts are theme variants of the same roles, not a second palette.

### Primary
- **Signal Blue**: Primary actions, keyboard focus, links, selected/on states, and MCP identification. Use the hover, soft-wash, and readable-ink variants for their established roles. The dark variant lifts lightness for the dark workbench without becoming a new accent.

### Secondary
- **Skill Cyan**: Small Skill dots, context-bar segments, and matrix legends. It identifies tool type; it is not a second action color.

### Tertiary
- **Plugin Violet**: Small Plugin dots, context-bar segments, and matrix legends. Keep it distinct from Signal Blue and out of ordinary buttons and navigation.

### Neutral
- **Graphite Mist**: The cool, slightly violet-neutral family behind page ground, sunk fields, solid cards, selected surfaces, overlays, four levels of text, and two hairline strengths. Surface and ink roles must remain semantic; do not hard-code a theme-specific step into a shared component.

### Status
- **Error Ember**, **Warning Amber**, and **Success Jade**: Each has a glyph color, readable text color, and soft surface. Error and warning badges use their soft surfaces; generic success badges remain neutral while the success glyph carries the green signal.

**The One Voice Rule.** Signal Blue is the only general action accent. Tool-type colors stay on small identity cues, and status colors communicate state rather than decorating the interface.

## Typography

**Body Font:** System sans (`-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `SF Pro Text`, `PingFang SC`, `Hiragino Sans GB`, `Noto Sans SC`, `Segoe UI`, sans-serif)

**Label/Mono Font:** System mono (`ui-monospace`, `SF Mono`, `JetBrains Mono`, `Menlo`, monospace)

**Character:** Compact sans text keeps controls and reading matter quiet; monospace paths, commands, metrics, chips, and table heads make technical information exact. There is no marketing display face in the workbench.

### Hierarchy
- **Title** (600, 15px, 1.375, -0.01em): The current page title; compact and aligned to the workbench rather than treated as a hero heading.
- **Dialog Title** (600, 14px, 1.5): Names a focused task without overpowering its contents.
- **Section Title** (600, 13px, 1.5): Panels, table regions, and grouped settings.
- **Body** (400, 13px, 1.5): Dense interface text and ordinary control content.
- **Reading Body** (400, 13px, 1.6): Alerts, empty states, explanations, and other sustained reading.
- **Control** (500, 13px): Buttons, navigation rows, and selectable agent tiles.
- **Compact** (12.5px): Segmented controls, activity text, and locally dense compositions.
- **Auxiliary** (400, 12px): Metadata, supporting descriptions, timestamps, and secondary rows.
- **Label** (500, 12px): Form labels and compact field guidance.
- **Caption** (500, 11px mono, 0.07em, uppercase): Table heads and workspace section headings.
- **Chip** (500, 11px mono, 0.02em): Tool-type identity and compact technical tags.
- **Status** (500, 11px mono, tabular numerals): Health badges and matrix cells.
- **Code** (400, 12px mono): Paths, repository names, commands, and diagnostic metadata.
- **Metric** (500, 22px mono, tabular numerals): KPI and health totals.

The browser base remains 16px/1.5; apply dense roles explicitly instead of assuming a global 13px reset. The generic `.cap` class currently lacks the mono assignment found on table and sidebar headings; that is implementation drift, not a second caption style.

**The Numeric Clarity Rule.** Counts, token totals, health metrics, and changing numeric columns use monospace with tabular numerals so scanning and updates do not disturb alignment.

## Layout

The desktop shell combines a 228px sticky sidebar, a 46px topbar, and a centered content column capped at 1280px. Main content uses 20px vertical padding, 16px horizontal padding below 768px, and 24px horizontal padding from 768px. Page-level stacks use a 16px gap.

The base spacing rhythm is 4px, with reused 2px micro-steps and 6/10/14/20/24px intermediate steps where components require them. Standard data rows are at least 44px high with 8px 14px padding and 12px gaps; table headers are 32px high. Buttons and inputs default to 32px, with 26px small buttons and 36px large controls.

At 640px, eligible forms gain a second column. At 768px, the desktop sidebar and topbar replace the mobile header; only one shell header is visible, and mobile navigation opens in a dialog rather than a persistent drawer. Below 1024px, Overview regroups each project into a stacked content-and-actions row, hides the six-column table header, and keeps actions visible for touch and keyboard users. From 1024px it returns to desktop table tracks with activity below; at 1400px there is enough room to place activity beside the table.

Library and Matrix preserve dense tracks with explicit horizontal scrolling on narrow screens. Library additionally caps its narrow master list at 144px with internal vertical scrolling so the selected detail and review action remain reachable without reversing DOM order. Add-tool previews put the diff before secondary metadata in both DOM and visual order; desktop uses diff-left and details-right, with a separator only when the panes are side by side. Generated file tabs form one horizontally scrollable row on narrow screens. At desktop widths with viewport height at or below 760px, the sidebar tightens its gap and scrolls.

Compact-density switching is a confirmed product requirement but is not implemented. Prototype compact measurements are not normative tokens.

## Elevation & Depth

Static surfaces are flat. Cards, tables, stat bars, and navigation create hierarchy through Graphite Mist tonal steps and 1px separators, not floating shadows. Dialogs, select menus, toasts, and pending bars use the overlay shadow; a selected standalone segment uses the subtle shadow. The switch thumb owns a local functional shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Overlay**: The dual-layer light/dark shadow reserved for transient surfaces above the workbench.
- **Subtle Selected**: A small single-layer shadow for the selected standalone segment only; it is not a card-hover treatment.

**The Flat-By-Default Rule.** Never add a shadow to a static card, table, navigation row, or stat bar. Elevation appears only when a transient overlay temporarily sits above the workflow.

## Shapes

Small, controlled radii keep the workbench precise: 4px keycaps and badges, 5px chips/statuses/options/matrix cells, 6px controls and navigation, 8px cards/tables/menus/toasts, and 12px dialogs. Pills and circles are reserved for switches, dots, and glyphs.

Default boundaries are 1px hairlines; stronger controls and overlays use the second line token. Pending matrix additions and removals use 1.5px dashed outlines. Focus uses a 2px Signal Blue outline with a 2px offset, while editable fields add an accent border and soft ring.

The declared 10px panel slot has no current consumer and is not normative. The exported `radius-cell` token means 6px even though the separate matrix-cell component uses 5px.

**The Small-Radius Rule.** Corners support dense grouping; they never turn panels into soft, oversized containers. Introduce a new radius only for a repeated component role.

## Components

### Buttons
Refined and restrained controls with a 32px default height, 12px horizontal padding, 6px radius, control typography, and a 6px icon-text gap. Primary uses Signal Blue with white text; secondary uses the solid surface and strong hairline; danger is transparent until its error-soft hover; text buttons gain a surface only on hover. Enabled presses move down 0.5px. Small buttons are 26px high and large buttons 36px. Disabled primary uses surface-3 and ink4; do not invent a global opacity treatment.

### Inputs / Fields
Fields are sunk into the field color with a 1px line border, 6px radius, 32px height, and 10px horizontal padding. Hover strengthens the border. Focus adds Signal Blue and a 2px soft ring alongside the global keyboard outline. Large fields are 36px high. Textareas use 8px 10px padding, 1.5 line height, and vertical resizing.

### Selects / Menus
The trigger reuses field styling, adds a 16px chevron, and uses an accent border while expanded. Menus sit 6px from the trigger, use the overlay surface, strong hairline, 8px radius, and overlay shadow. Options are at least 30px high with 5px radius; active options use accent-soft and selected options use accent-ink with medium weight. Menus flip upward when space requires it.

### Chips / Status Badges
Tool chips are non-interactive, 20px-high ghost labels with a strong hairline, 5px radius, and monospace text. Skill, MCP, and Plugin variants add a 6px type-color dot; agent chips switch to sans and omit the dot. Error and warning status badges use their soft status surfaces. Status color is paired with a glyph or text, never used alone.

### Cards / Stat Bars
Cards and tables use solid surface, line border, and normally 8px radius; there is no glass material or card-hover lift. Project columns use 12px 14px header and row padding. Stat bars are one divided grid: 1px gaps expose the line token between surface-colored segments, so separators follow both wrapped columns and rows. Segments use 12px 16px padding, compact captions, and 22px mono metrics. Doctor retains three numeric columns; below 640px each stat places glyph/count first and its full-width label second.

### Tables / Dense Rows
Rows favor alignment and scanning over decoration. Standard rows are at least 44px high with 8px 14px padding. Hover rows use surface-2. The selected Library row adds a 2px inset Signal Blue edge; that treatment is not the current sidebar-active pattern. Row actions appear on both hover and focus-within. Dense tracks scroll horizontally where implemented; current Matrix headers are not sticky.

### Navigation
The desktop sidebar is 228px wide, solid, and divided from the page by a hairline. Links are 30px high with 6px radius, 13px text, and small Lucide icons. Hover and active states use surface-2 with primary ink; active text gains medium weight. There is no active accent bar in the current sidebar. On mobile, navigation moves into a dialog.

### Dialogs / Toasts / Pending Bars
Dialogs use the overlay surface, strong hairline, 12px radius, and overlay shadow. SimpleDialog headers and bodies use 14px 16px padding; footers use 12px 16px and wrap their actions. When no control requests autofocus, the dialog container receives initial focus: forward Tab enters the first control and backward Tab wraps to the last, so neither direction escapes into the background. Generic and simple dialogs have different maximum widths, so width remains component-specific. Toasts use 8px radius, 12px 16px padding, and a 3px status-specific left border. Pending bars combine explanatory text with exactly one reset and one apply action.

### Matrix Cells
Matrix cells are 26×26px with 5px radius and monospace glyphs. On cells are Signal Blue with a white check; off cells are transparent with a strong hairline. Pending add/remove states use dashed accent/error outlines and soft washes. Unavailable cells use a repeating diagonal neutral pattern. Enabled hover scales to 1.12 and active to 0.96; reduced motion removes the hover transform.

### Context Visualization
Context bars use compact Skill/MCP/Plugin segments on a neutral track, normally 6px high with 3px clipping and 2px segment gaps. Unmeasured capacity uses a diagonal repeating pattern. Segment width may transition for 300ms; legends use small type dots and readable labels rather than color alone.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** build hierarchy from semantic Graphite Mist surfaces, ink levels, and 1px separators.
- **Do** reserve Signal Blue for actions, focus, links, and selected/on states.
- **Do** use monospace and tabular numerals for paths, commands, metrics, and changing counts.
- **Do** preserve both semantic light and dark token variants in shared components.
- **Do** keep state transitions brief and direct, with reduced-motion alternatives for entrance and transform effects.
- **Do** keep empty states text-led and status signals paired with glyphs or labels.
- **Do** verify interface changes against the confirmed WCAG 2.2 AA product target.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use decorative gradients, content-layer glass, blur, or illustration-led empty states; the ATM logo gradient is brand artwork only.
- **Don't** add shadows to static cards, tables, stat bars, or navigation rows.
- **Don't** turn Skill Cyan, Plugin Violet, or status colors into general action accents or large background washes.
- **Don't** introduce a marketing display face, oversized hero typography, or staggered motion into the workbench.
- **Don't** treat prototype-only compact density, global ⌘K chrome, sticky Matrix headers, or sidebar accent bars as implemented behavior.
- **Don't** communicate health or tool type through color alone.
- **Don't** copy legacy Liquid Glass values, unused radius/motion slots, or one-off hard-coded colors into the normative system.
